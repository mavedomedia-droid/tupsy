Questions

Things people ask us.

Do I need to have my own stock ready before applying?

No. We produce the stock with you, or you supply it — either works. All we need to start is the finished units sitting in our warehouse by the time your page goes live. The minimum backlog keeps you from selling something you can't ship.

How is the 5% calculated — before or after shipping?

5% is on the product retail price only. Shipping is not part of the split — it's charged separately to the customer at checkout, and it covers the actual cost of delivery only. So neither you nor Tupsy pays for shipping, and it never reduces your payout.

Who pays for shipping?

The customer does. When they check out, the delivery fee is calculated based on their location and added to their total — on top of the product price. That fee passes through at cost. It isn't a revenue line for us and it isn't a cost for you.

When do I get paid?

Within 7 working days of each order shipping. You'll get an email with the sale details and the exact amount paid to your account.

Can I sell more than one product?

Yes. We start with one sample piece so we can see your style and pricing, then add the rest together during onboarding. Every additional piece follows the same 95/5 split and stock backlog rules.

Can I change my prices later?

Absolutely. Just message us and we'll update them. Changes take effect immediately on your storefront.

Who handles returns and refunds?

Tupsy handles the return logistics. Refunds are paid back to the customer, and the 5% on that sale is reversed — so you only keep the 95% on sales that stick. Shipping is refunded at cost the same way it was charged.
