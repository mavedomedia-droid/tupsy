Five steps from the first message to something people can wear.

  1. 01

    Start a Project

    Tell us a little about what you're looking to make.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    We'll talk it through properly — concept, garments, materials, sizing, quantities, budget and timeline.

    Our lead fashion designer, Fay, brings her fashion and garment-development experience into the process.
  3. 03

    Sample Development

    We'll agree on the sample, send you a quote and timeline, and get to work.

  4. 04

    Production

    Once you're happy with the sample, we agree on your first production quantity and get it made.

  5. 05

    Sell It — Optional

    Take your merch and sell it yourself, or let Tupsy host, sell and fulfil it for you.
